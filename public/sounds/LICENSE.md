# Mechanical mouse click

- Original: **Mouse clicks**, Breviceps, 14 November 2018.
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/447938/
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/447/447938_9159316-hq.mp3
- Modification: isolated 0.742–0.827 seconds of the recording (one switch click), mixed to mono, normalized peak to 0.72, added sub-millisecond entry and 3 ms exit fades, exported 16-bit PCM WAV at 44.1 kHz.

`src/assets/sounds/mouse-click.wav` is a real recorded transient, not an electronic beep. Playback is attenuated to 45% in `useUISound`. The original recording and conversion dependencies are not required by the application.
